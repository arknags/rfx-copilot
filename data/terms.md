# FY27 Corrugated Sheet Supply RFx — Commercial Terms

1. Quote every item you can supply and identify the corresponding buyer item ID where possible. Suppliers may respond in their own document format.
2. State price currency, price basis, pack size, minimum order quantity, and whether the price is per sheet, per bundle, per 100 sheets, per kilogram, or another basis.
3. Quote prices excluding GST. Identify freight, unloading, tooling, printing plate, and any other charges separately.
4. Delivery location is the buyer warehouse in Chennai. State lead time and delivery terms.
5. Offer validity must be at least 30 days from RFx close.
6. State payment or credit terms and available monthly production capacity.
7. The buyer may split the award across suppliers and may seek clarification on ambiguous commercial information.
8. Quality qualification requires affirmative responses to Q01, Q02, Q03, and Q04. Supporting documentation may be attached in any form.
9. Final award remains subject to technical verification, sample approval, and commercial review. This RFx is not a purchase order.
